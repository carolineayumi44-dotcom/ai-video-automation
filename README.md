Dawn: "Segunda eu começo" (Ep. 1)

💡 The concept

"Segunda eu começo" ("I'll start on Monday") is something everyone says, and the snooze button is where it falls apart.

The video is a split-screen comparison of the same morning, side by side:

Top: with Dawn	Bottom: with a regular alarm
Gets up on time: the alarm only turns off after a challenge ("Dance!")	5:30 · snooze
Squats with Floris, his little nephew → "Mission complete"	5:45 · snooze x3
Breakfast together	5:50 · Floris gives up waiting
Takes Floris to daycare	6:58 · still asleep
On the way, in the neighborhood	7:40 · late

30 days later: Floris performs on stage at the daycare party. In one version, Lucas is in the audience filming him. In the other, there's an empty chair with a "Tio Lucas" sign.

Closing line:

"5 minutos a mais na cama. Um momento a menos com ele." ("5 more minutes in bed. One less moment with him.")

Ending card: Dawn logo · "o alarme que você não consegue ignorar" · "Ep. 2 amanhã", setting up a series to keep viewers coming back.

🎯 Strategy
Hook in the first second: the relatable "Segunda eu começo" + a phone full of backup alarms (5:35, 5:40 "agora sim", 5:45 "agora vai", 5:50 "último mesmo")
Split-screen storytelling: "mesmos 5 minutos" makes the cost of snoozing visible without explaining it
Emotional payoff instead of a feature list: the product (challenge-to-dismiss alarm) appears naturally inside the story
Series format (Ep. 1): built for recurring posts and follower retention
Localized for Brazil: setting, characters, food (café com pão na chapa) and language made for a Brazilian audience
